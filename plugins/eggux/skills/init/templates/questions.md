# Questions

The one list of open questions in this project: unknowns from discovery, questions a feature
cannot answer yet, accepted research findings, blindspots. Each gets options; the chosen one
becomes a decision in decisions.md and a task in tasks.md. `/eggux:board` shows them in Decide.

Format: `## Q-001 · Question` with `- Type: unknown | finding | blindspot`, `- Source:`
(`discovery:<file>`, `feature:<slug>`, `research:<slug> F-01`), `- Category:`, `- Status: open`,
optional `- Blocks:` and `- Recommended: A`; options as `### A · Title` with `- Pro:`, `- Con:`,
`- By: claude | you`.
