# Checkout patterns on mobile

## Report

### What was analysed
Six public sources on mobile checkout: two Baymard studies, the Apple and Material guidance on touch targets, and three European e-commerce case studies.

### What we found
On mobile, the biggest drop-off is the address form. People abandon when they have to type many fields on a phone keyboard. Address autocomplete (from the postcode or from suggestions) cut completion time by almost half in both case studies.

Small buttons are the second problem. Five of the six sources recommend a touch target of at least 44 px, and three recommend keeping the primary button in the thumb zone, low on the screen.

Button colour appears in only one source, with no data showing an effect on conversion.

### Limits
These are general studies, not the client's data. Confirm the findings against the current checkout analytics.

## Findings

### F-01 · Buttons need to be bigger
- Source: 5 of 6 sources: touch target of at least 44 px on mobile
- Vote: accepted

### F-02 · Buttons should be blue
