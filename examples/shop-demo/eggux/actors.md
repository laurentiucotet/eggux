# Actors

## Busy parent (shopper)
- **Who:** buys from a phone, in short gaps between other tasks; a few times a month.
- **Goals:** finish an order in under 2 minutes; never create an account just to pay; fast delivery.
- **Permissions:** browse, check out as guest or with an account, manage own account and orders.
- **Constraints:** one hand free, weak mobile connection, forgets passwords.

## Loyal customer
- **Who:** has an account, orders monthly, mostly on mobile.
- **Goals:** reorder from history, collect loyalty points.
- **Permissions:** everything a shopper can do, plus saved addresses, order history, points.
- **Constraints:** rarely signs in on a new device, so often does not remember the password.

## Support agent
- **Who:** shop staff answering customer emails and chat.
- **Goals:** solve account problems without asking for passwords.
- **Permissions:** see customer accounts and orders; send a password reset email; cannot see or set passwords.
- **Constraints:** desktop, many tickets per hour.
