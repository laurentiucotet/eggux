# Brand

A sample client used to show the EGG UX workflow end to end: an online shop for home products,
redesigning its mobile checkout and account flows. All facts here are invented examples.

## Identity
- Logo: none (sample)
- Personality: calm, direct, helpful
- Voice: informal "you", short sentences, no jargon. Errors say what happened and what to do
  next, without blame or apologies.
